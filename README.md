Most PMs are learning AI in the wrong order.

They try to master tools.

Claude Code. n8n. Codex. Lovable.

Sure, you need tools to build intuition and generalize. But tools change every month.

Here's the roadmap that lasts:

1. Foundations
Understand what models can and can't do.

2. Knowledge systems
Design how agents know things.

3. Agents on your own work
Use them to research, prototype, and ship.

4. Agents inside your product
Build agents that run for users and teams.

5. Trust and shipping
Make them reliable when they run without you.

6. Strategy and leadership
Turn AI capability into product advantage.

You learn those foundations once.
